Cookie policy

Cookies and similar technologies.

This page explains how Memory Tune uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies when you browse the site, create a song, recover your library, or complete payment.

Last updated: 14 May 2026

What we use them for

Memory Tune currently uses only technologies that are needed to run the service, keep your session working, support checkout, and help you recover your songs.

Strictly necessary technologies

These may be used without prior consent where they are essential to deliver the service you actively request.

  • Keeping track of your current song-building session.
  • Helping you recover your previews or paid songs from the same device.
  • Supporting secure payment return flows and checkout continuity.
  • Remembering basic site functionality and security-related choices.

In practice, this site currently uses browser storage such as localStorage for session and library continuity, and payment providers such as Stripe may place their own essential cookies during checkout.

Advertising and measurement cookies

Memory Tune uses the Meta Pixel to understand visits and campaign performance and to measure events such as page views, lead generation, checkout initiation, and completed purchases. We do not currently describe the live service as using Google Analytics.

Managing your choices

  • You can clear browser cookies and local storage through your browser settings.
  • If a consent banner or preferences tool is active, you can revisit it to change your choices.
  • Blocking essential technologies may prevent checkout, session recovery, or library access from working correctly.

Third-party services

Some third parties connected to the service, such as Stripe or content delivery tools, may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Where their tools are essential to provide the service you request, those technologies may operate as part of the transaction flow.

Privacy and performance

Cookies are only one part of the picture. Your wider personal-data rights are explained in the privacy policy, including lawful bases, retention, and your rights under UK GDPR.

If this changes later

If Memory Tune later expands beyond the current Meta Pixel setup to add further optional analytics, advertising, or remarketing technologies, the site should then add any additional consent controls required for those tools.
